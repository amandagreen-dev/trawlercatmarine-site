TrawlerCat Marine is a small boatyard on the Treasure Coast. We build twin-hull powerboats to order, and we also do hull repairs, engine room refits and seasonal haul-out work for local owners. Most jobs start with a conversation about how you use the boat. Send us an email with your boat and what you need, and we will reply with a written quote.
